Текст · B1

Why cities are planting more trees

Many cities are planting trees to make streets more comfortable. Trees provide shade, and their leaves can cool the air. In one city, researchers compared two streets during a warm summer. The street with more trees was cooler in the afternoon. Residents also said they preferred walking there.

However, trees need space and regular care. Young trees may not survive if they do not receive enough water. City planners therefore choose species that can grow in local conditions. They also ask residents where shade is most needed.

Практика тренирует точность, не выставляет официальный band.
